# Rating guide — IELTS Writing Task 2

Thank you for rating. Your scores are the reference that BandUp's automated
scoring is measured against, so consistency matters more than speed.

## What you need

- The **official IELTS Writing Task 2 band descriptors (public version)**,
  published by IELTS (British Council / IDP / Cambridge). Rate against those —
  not against any wording from BandUp.
- The essays file you were sent, and a copy of `ratings.template.csv`.

## How to rate

1. **Calibrate first.** Rate the anchor essays you were sent before anything
   else. We'll compare scores with the other raters and discuss differences
   before independent rating starts.
2. **Rate independently.** Don't discuss essays with other raters or look at
   any other scores (including automated ones) until you've submitted.
3. **Score the four criteria as whole bands (0–9):**
   - `task_response`
   - `coherence_cohesion`
   - `lexical_resource`
   - `grammatical_range_accuracy`

   Don't calculate an overall score — it's derived from your four bands.
4. **Apply the test rules:** penalise under-length responses (fewer than 250
   words) under Task Response; a largely off-topic or memorised response
   can't score above band 5 for Task Response.
5. **Add one short justification** covering the four criteria (one clause
   each is enough). Paraphrase rather than quoting the essay.
6. **Rate in one or two sittings per batch** of about 20 essays, and take
   breaks — fatigue causes drift.

## Filling in the sheet

One row per essay, using your rater ID (e.g. `R1`) on every row:

```
essay_id,rater_id,task_response,coherence_cohesion,lexical_resource,grammatical_range_accuracy,justification
W001,R1,6,6,6,5,"TR: clear position, second view thinly developed. CC: ..."
```

Use whole numbers only. If an essay is unreadable or clearly not a genuine
attempt, leave the scores blank and explain in the justification.

## Confidentiality

Essays are shared with the writers' consent for evaluation only. Don't copy,
share or keep them after rating, and delete your local copies when the batch
is complete.
